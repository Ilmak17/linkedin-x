The one thing

Someone opens this once and thinks: oh, these are people writing, not brands posting. Every decision below serves that sentence.

Safe: single 600px column, avatar left, actions bottom

The correct structure for a timeline. Borrowed from x.com because it works, not because it's fashionable.

Risk: post body set in a serif

Reframes a LinkedIn post from ad copy into writing. Costs some app-ness; all chrome stays in the sans to compensate.

Risk: amber accent, no blue anywhere

LinkedIn is blue. X is blue. Amber reads as a reading lamp and makes the product recognizable in one glance.

Risk: hairlines only, zero cards

Removes the corporate card-stack read entirely. Costs boundary clarity between short posts; padding carries it.
